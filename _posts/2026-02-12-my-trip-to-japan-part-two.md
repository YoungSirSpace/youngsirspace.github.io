After a comfortable sleep, I was shocked by the beautiful blue sky as soon as I woke up. The sky was a clear, dust-free blue, like there was someone painted with the transparent blue pigment, which made the sky both close at hand and infinitely deep. Before coming to Japan, I had heard that the sky there was very blue. The scene before me proved that the rumor was true.

Finished the breakfast, we set up for the Osaka Castle, a famous Tenshukaku.
